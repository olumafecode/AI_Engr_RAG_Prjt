# Staff Separation and Termination Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how employment with Veridane Bank ends, whether the staff member or the bank ends it. It also sets out the exit steps that protect the bank, its customers, and the departing staff member.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff members. The exit process in section 13 also applies to contractors when their assignment ends; the contracting department manages it together with HR.

## 3. Types of Separation

- Resignation
- Retirement
- End of a fixed-term contract
- Redundancy
- Termination for poor performance
- Termination for misconduct, with notice
- Summary dismissal for gross misconduct, without notice
- Job abandonment
- Death in service

## 4. Notice Periods

| Situation | Notice required from either side |
|---|---|
| During probation (first 6 months) | 2 weeks |
| Band A | 3 months |
| Band B | 2 months |
| Bands C and D | 1 month |
| Band E | 2 weeks |

- The bank may pay salary in lieu of all or part of the notice period.
- The bank may place a staff member on garden leave for all or part of the notice period. During garden leave the staff member remains employed and paid but does not come to work or access bank systems.
- Staff in the sensitive roles listed in VB-POL-006 are moved to non-sensitive duties, or placed on garden leave, from the day their resignation is accepted or notice is given. During the notice period they do not handle cash, hold vault combinations, or authorize payments.

## 5. Resignation

- A staff member resigns in writing through the HR portal. The line manager acknowledges the resignation within 2 business days.
- Once a resignation has been accepted, it can be withdrawn only with the agreement of the line manager and HR.
- HR offers an exit interview to every staff member who resigns (section 16).

## 6. Retirement

- The normal retirement point is age 60 or 35 years of service, whichever comes first.
- HR gives written notice at least 6 months before the retirement date.
- Early retirement from age 55 is possible with the CHRO's approval.

## 7. End of a Fixed-Term Contract

HR tells the staff member at least 1 month before the contract end date whether the contract will be renewed.

## 8. Redundancy

- Redundancy applies when a role is no longer needed because of restructuring, a branch closure, or a change in technology.
- Redundancies require EXCO approval. Any redundancy program affecting more than 20 staff is also reported to the Board Nominations and Remuneration Committee.
- HR consults affected staff and looks for suitable alternative roles in the bank before confirming a redundancy.
- Severance pay is 2 weeks' basic salary for each completed year of service, with a minimum of 1 month's basic salary. It is paid in addition to notice pay and pay for unused annual leave.

## 9. Termination for Poor Performance

A staff member may be terminated for poor performance only after a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) of at least 90 days, with clear targets, regular reviews, and support. The decision is made by the Head of Department together with HR. The staff member may appeal under VB-POL-004, section 16.5.

## 10. Termination for Misconduct and Summary Dismissal

### 10.1 Process

Misconduct is handled under the disciplinary procedure in VB-POL-004, section 16. That procedure covers investigation, written allegations, a hearing, and the right of appeal. The decision-maker depends on the staff member's band (VB-POL-004, section 16.4).

### 10.2 Summary dismissal

Gross misconduct, as defined in VB-POL-004, section 16.1, may lead to summary dismissal. Employment ends immediately, without notice or pay in lieu of notice. The staff member receives salary up to the dismissal date and pay for accrued unused annual leave, minus any amounts they owe the bank. No bonus is paid.

### 10.3 Removal from the premises

A staff member who is summarily dismissed, or suspended pending investigation, may be escorted from the premises. HR notifies IT immediately, and all system access is disabled within 4 hours of HR's notification (VB-POL-002, section 9.3).

### 10.4 Whistleblower protection check

For 24 months after a staff member makes a named whistleblowing report, HR must consult the CCO before taking any disciplinary or termination action against them (VB-POL-005, section 7.4).

### 10.5 Regulatory reporting and re-employment

Dismissals for fraud or forgery are included in the monthly frauds and forgeries return to the CMA (VB-POL-006, section 9). Staff dismissed for gross misconduct are not eligible for re-employment.

## 11. Job Abandonment

When a staff member has been absent for 5 consecutive business days without contacting the bank (VB-POL-009, section 11), HR tries to reach them by phone and email and sends a written notice to their last known address. If the staff member does not respond within 5 business days of the notice, their employment ends on the last day they attended work.

## 12. Death in Service

HR contacts the next of kin within 2 business days to offer support. The bank pays the staff member's estate the full salary for the month in which the death occurred and pay for accrued unused annual leave. It also pays a death-in-service benefit of 3 times annual basic salary to the nominated beneficiary.

## 13. Exit Process

### 13.1 Access removal

- HR notifies IT at least 5 business days before the last working day. All access is disabled by the end of the last working day (VB-POL-002, section 9.3).
- Passwords for any service accounts known to the leaver are changed within 5 business days of their departure (VB-POL-002, section 4.1).
- Disabled accounts are deleted after 90 days. Mailbox content is kept for 7 years (VB-POL-003).

### 13.2 Signatory rights and authorities

By the last working day, the line manager arranges for the leaver to be removed from authorized signatory lists, mandates, CoreLink authorization limits, and any lists held by correspondent banks or vendors.

### 13.3 Cash and vault custodians

- Branch staff who hold cash hand it over under dual control, and the BOM verifies it before they leave.
- If a vault custodian leaves or is removed from custodian duties, the vault combination is changed on the same day (VB-POL-008).

### 13.4 Return of bank property

By the last working day, the leaver returns their laptop, phone, hardware MFA token, staff access card, keys, bank documents, and uniform items that carry the bank's logo. The replacement cost of items not returned may be deducted from final pay, as far as the law allows.

### 13.5 Personal devices

Bank data on personal devices enrolled under the Remote and Flexible Work Policy (VB-POL-013) is removed by selective wipe on the last working day. The staff member's personal data on the device is not affected.

### 13.6 Clearance and final pay

The leaver's clearance form is signed by the line manager, IT, Finance, Facilities, and HR. Final pay, including salary, unused annual leave, and any other amounts due, is paid in the next payroll after clearance is complete, and no later than 30 days after the last working day.

### 13.7 Staff loans and cards

Outstanding staff loans and staff credit cards convert to standard customer terms 30 days after the last working day unless they are repaid.

## 14. Obligations After Leaving

- The duty of confidentiality continues after a staff member leaves (VB-POL-004, section 9). Leavers must not keep or take any bank information.
- Band A and Band B staff must not solicit the bank's customers or staff for 6 months after leaving.

## 15. References and Service Certificates

- Only HR gives references on behalf of the bank. A bank reference confirms dates of employment and job title only.
- Line managers must not give references on the bank's behalf. Any personal reference must be clearly marked as personal.
- HR issues a service certificate within 10 business days of a request.

## 16. Exit Interviews

HR offers an exit interview to every staff member who leaves voluntarily. Answers are confidential. HR reports anonymized themes to EXCO every quarter.

## 17. Announcing Departures

Departures of Band A and Band B staff are announced by Corporate Communications after CEO approval (VB-POL-011). Other departures are communicated to the team by the line manager.

## 18. Records

Personnel files are kept for 7 years after the staff member leaves (VB-POL-003).
