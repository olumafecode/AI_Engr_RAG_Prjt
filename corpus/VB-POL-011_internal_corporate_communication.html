1. Purpose

What Veridane Bank says, and who says it, affects customer trust and the bank's reputation. This policy sets out who may speak for the bank, how customers and staff are kept informed, how social media is used, and how the bank communicates during a crisis.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff members and to every communication made in the bank's name. That includes media statements, social media, the website, advertising, customer notices, in-app messages, and internal announcements.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

  • Head of Corporate Communications (HCC): owns this policy, manages media relations and the bank's official social media accounts, and approves all public statements.
  • CEO: is the bank's chief spokesperson and approves statements on major incidents.
  • Line managers: pass on (cascade) internal announcements to their teams and make sure staff know this policy.
  • Compliance: reviews advertising and customer notices to make sure they are fair, clear, and not misleading.
  • All staff: refer media enquiries to Corporate Communications and follow the social media rules in section 6.

4. Authorized Spokespersons

Only the people below may speak to the media or make public statements for the bank, and only on the topics listed.

TopicAuthorized spokesperson
Bank strategy, performance, and major incidentsCEO
Financial resultsCEO and CFO
Digital channels, digital products, and digital outagesCDO
Security incidents and data breachesCEO, supported by the CISO and the DPO
Branch and regional mattersRegional Manager, with HCC approval for each engagement
All other media enquiriesHCC

The CIO/CTO, the CISO, and the DPO speak to the media only with the CEO's approval. Spokespersons complete media training every 2 years.

5. Media Relations

  • A staff member contacted by a journalist must not comment. They should say: "I'm not able to comment, but I'll pass your details to our Corporate Communications team." They must forward the enquiry to media@veridane.example within 1 hour.
  • Every press release is approved by the HCC. Press releases about financial results are also approved by the CFO and the CEO. Press releases about incidents are also approved by the CEO and the General Counsel.
  • Journalists may enter bank premises, and filming or photography may take place in branches, only with the HCC's approval.
  • After a robbery or other serious incident at a branch, all media enquiries go to Corporate Communications. Branch staff do not make any statement (VB-POL-008).

6. Social Media

6.1 Official accounts

  • The bank's official social media accounts are managed by Corporate Communications.
  • The DFS social care team answers customer service questions on the official accounts, following Corporate Communications guidelines. It responds within 2 hours between 7:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m.
  • No staff member may create an account that uses the bank's name or logo without the HCC's approval.

6.2 Personal use by staff

Staff may say on their personal profiles that they work for Veridane Bank. When they do, they must not:

  • post confidential information, customer information, or anything about a customer's account;
  • post photos or videos taken in the back office, the vault, data centers, or any area showing screens or documents;
  • comment on bank incidents, outages, or robberies;
  • reply to customer complaints online. Staff should point the customer to official channels instead;
  • criticize customers or colleagues, or post anything that breaches VB-POL-004.

Staff who discuss the banking industry on personal accounts must make clear that the views are their own.

6.3 Fake accounts

Staff who see a social media account or page impersonating the bank must report it to Corporate Communications and the Fraud Desk.

7. Customer Communications

7.1 Notices and timelines

SituationRequirement
Changes to fees, charges, or account termsCustomers notified at least 30 days before the change takes effect
Emergency branch closureNotice at the branch door, a Veridane Mobile notification, and a website update within 1 hour of the RM's approval
Planned maintenance outside the standard Sunday window (1:00 a.m. to 3:00 a.m.)At least 48 hours' notice in Veridane Mobile, Veridane Online, and on the website
Unplanned digital channel outage lasting more than 30 minutesFirst update on the website and official social media within 30 minutes of confirming the outage, then updates every 60 minutes until service is restored
Personal data breach affecting customersNotice drafted by the DPO with Corporate Communications and approved by the CEO (VB-POL-003)

7.2 Approval

All customer notices and in-app messages are approved by Corporate Communications and Compliance before they are sent.

7.3 Official senders and fraud warnings

  • The bank sends SMS only from the sender ID "VERIDANE" and email only from veridane.example addresses.
  • Every customer email and SMS campaign reminds customers that the bank will never ask for their PIN, one-time passcode, or password.
  • Customers are told to report suspicious messages to the Fraud Desk.

8. Internal Communications

  • Veridane Intranet: the official source of bank news, announcements, and current policies. The Company Secretary publishes policies there (VB-ORG-001, section 8.2), and Corporate Communications announces changes.
  • All-staff email: only Corporate Communications may send to the all-staff distribution list. Requests must be submitted at least 2 business days in advance. The CISO and the SOC may send urgent security alerts directly.
  • CEO town hall: held every quarter, with a monthly CEO video update on the intranet.
  • Branch morning huddle: every branch holds a 10-minute huddle at 7:35 a.m., led by the BM or the BOM.
  • Cascading: line managers pass on EXCO decisions that affect staff within 5 business days of receiving the Corporate Communications briefing.
  • Engagement survey: an all-staff engagement survey runs every October.
  • Internal communications are classified as Internal unless labeled otherwise. Confidential matters must not be sent in all-staff messages.

8.1 Appointments and departures

Appointments and departures of Band A and Band B staff are announced by Corporate Communications after CEO approval (VB-POL-010). Band A appointments are announced externally only after the CMA has approved them.

9. Crisis Communications

9.1 Crisis Communications Team

The team is led by the HCC and includes the CEO, the General Counsel, the CRO, and the executive responsible for the issue: the CISO for cyber incidents, the CDO for digital outages, and the COO for branch incidents.

9.2 When the team is activated

  • Severity 1 security incidents (VB-POL-001, section 12.3)
  • Robberies or other serious incidents at branches
  • Digital channel outages lasting more than 2 hours
  • Fraud or data breaches likely to become public
  • Any other event likely to attract significant media attention

9.3 Response

  • A holding statement is issued within 2 hours of the team being activated. It is approved by the CEO and the General Counsel.
  • Where possible, staff are briefed internally no later than the external statement is released.
  • Notifications to regulators, such as the CMA, the DPC, and the FIB, are made by the responsible officers (the CISO, the DPO, or the MLRO), not by Corporate Communications. The HCC makes sure public messages are consistent with those notifications.

10. Brand and Advertising

  • The bank's logo and brand may be used only in line with the brand guidelines published on the intranet.
  • All advertising is approved by Corporate Communications and Compliance. Advertisements that mention interest rates must show the annual percentage rate.
  • Promotional material in branches and digital hubs is removed within 2 business days after a campaign ends.

11. Speaking Engagements and Publications

Staff who wish to speak at a conference or publish an article about banking or about the bank must get the HCC's approval at least 10 business days before the event or publication. Engagements by Band A staff are also approved by the CEO.

12. Political Neutrality

The bank does not make political donations or endorse any party or candidate. Staff must not suggest that the bank supports any political position (VB-POL-004, section 14).

13. Records

Corporate Communications keeps copies of all press releases and official public statements for 7 years.

14. Compliance and Enforcement

Unauthorized media statements, and breaches of the social media rules, are handled under the disciplinary procedure in VB-POL-004.
