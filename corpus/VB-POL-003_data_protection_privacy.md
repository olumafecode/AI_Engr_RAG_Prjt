# Data Protection and Privacy Policy

## 1. Purpose

Veridane Bank collects and uses personal data about customers, staff, and other people in order to provide banking services and run the bank. This policy sets out how the bank handles that data lawfully, fairly, and securely, and how it meets the requirements of data protection law and the Data Protection Commission (DPC).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff members, contractors, and vendors who handle personal data on the bank's behalf. It covers personal data about:

- customers and prospective customers, including users of Veridane Mobile, Veridane Online, and Veridane Wallet;
- guarantors, beneficiaries, and other people connected to a customer's account;
- staff members, former staff, and job applicants;
- contact people at suppliers and business partners;
- visitors to bank premises, including people recorded on CCTV.

It covers personal data in any form, including paper files, systems, emails, call recordings, and images.

## 3. Definitions

- **Personal data:** any information that identifies a living person or could be used to identify them, such as a name, account number, phone number, card number, device ID, or location data.
- **Sensitive personal data:** personal data about health, biometrics (including fingerprints and facial images used for identity verification), religious or philosophical beliefs, ethnic origin, trade union membership, criminal records, or sex life. Sensitive personal data is classified as Restricted under VB-POL-001.
- **Processing:** anything done with personal data, including collecting, storing, using, sharing, and deleting it.
- **Data subject:** the person the personal data is about.
- **Processor:** an organization that processes personal data on the bank's instructions, such as a card production vendor or a cloud provider.
- **Personal data breach:** a security incident that leads to personal data being lost, destroyed, altered, disclosed, or accessed without authorization.

## 4. Data Protection Principles

All processing of personal data must follow these principles:

1. **Lawful, fair, and transparent:** there is a lawful basis for the processing, and data subjects are told what happens to their data.
2. **Purpose limitation:** data is collected for specified purposes and not used for unrelated ones.
3. **Data minimization:** only the data needed for the purpose is collected.
4. **Accuracy:** data is kept accurate and up to date.
5. **Storage limitation:** data is kept no longer than the retention periods in section 10.
6. **Security:** data is protected against unauthorized access, loss, and damage, in line with VB-POL-001.
7. **Accountability:** the bank can show that it complies with these principles.

## 5. Lawful Bases for Processing

The bank processes personal data only when at least one of these bases applies:

| Lawful basis | Examples |
|---|---|
| Performance of a contract | Opening and running accounts, issuing cards, processing payments |
| Legal or regulatory obligation | KYC checks, suspicious transaction reporting, tax reporting, regulatory returns |
| Legitimate interest | Fraud monitoring on cards and digital channels, CCTV at branches and ATMs, network security monitoring |
| Consent | Direct marketing, sharing account data with third-party providers through open banking APIs, optional app features such as location-based offers |

Where processing relies on consent, the consent must be freely given, specific, recorded, and as easy to withdraw as it was to give.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Board Risk Committee:** approves this policy and receives an annual data protection report from the DPO.
- **DPO:** owns this policy, advises on data protection, handles data subject requests, keeps the breach register, signs off data protection impact assessments, and is the bank's contact point with the DPC. The DPO reports to the CCO and may raise concerns directly with the BRC Chair.
- **Data owners:** Band B heads accountable for a system or dataset. They make sure it is used only for approved purposes and kept for the right period.
- **Chief Digital Officer:** makes sure DFS channels, apps, and APIs are designed and run in line with this policy.
- **All staff:** handle personal data only as their role requires and report suspected breaches within one hour.

## 7. Privacy Notices

Data subjects are told how their data is used through privacy notices:

- Customers receive the Customer Privacy Notice at account opening, whether in a branch, through Veridane Mobile, or through Veridane Online. The mobile app shows the notice before registration is completed.
- Staff receive the Staff Privacy Notice with their offer letter.
- CCTV signs are displayed at every branch entrance, ATM site, and digital hub.

The DPO reviews all privacy notices every year, and before any new product or channel that changes how personal data is used.

## 8. Data Subject Rights

Data subjects have the right to:

- access a copy of their personal data;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have data deleted where the bank no longer has a lawful reason to keep it;
- object to direct marketing at any time;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive data they provided in a portable format.

### 8.1 Handling requests

- A request may arrive through any channel: a branch, the DFS Contact Center, email, or letter. The staff member who receives it must forward it to the DPO (dpo@veridane.example) within 1 business day. Staff must not respond to the request themselves.
- The DPO verifies the requester's identity before releasing any data.
- The bank responds within 30 days of receiving the request. For complex requests the DPO may extend this by a further 30 days and must tell the requester about the extension within the first 30 days.
- Requests are free of charge. The DPO may charge a reasonable fee for, or refuse, a request that is manifestly unfounded or excessive, and must record the reason.
- A request to delete data does not override the retention periods required by law in section 10.

## 9. Direct Marketing

- Marketing messages are sent only to customers who have opted in.
- Every marketing message includes a simple way to opt out. Opt-outs made through Veridane Mobile, Veridane Online, a branch, or the DFS Contact Center are applied within 3 business days.
- Marketing SMS and push notifications are sent only between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.
- The bank does not send marketing to customers under 18 years of age.
- Service messages, such as transaction alerts and security notices, are not marketing and are sent regardless of marketing preferences.

## 10. Records Retention Schedule

| Record | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Customer account records and statements | 7 years after the account is closed |
| KYC and customer due diligence documents | 7 years after the customer relationship ends |
| Transaction records (all channels) | 7 years after the transaction |
| Loan files | 7 years after the loan is fully repaid or written off |
| Card dispute and chargeback files | 5 years after the dispute is closed |
| Contact center call recordings | 2 years after the call |
| CCTV footage | 90 days, unless kept for an investigation |
| Staff personnel files | 7 years after the staff member leaves |
| Mailboxes of departed staff | 7 years after the staff member leaves |
| Unsuccessful job applications | 12 months after the recruitment decision |
| Marketing consent records | For as long as the consent is active, plus 2 years |
| Security logs | 7 years (see VB-POL-001, section 13) |

When a retention period ends, records are securely deleted or shredded. Vendors that destroy records for the bank must provide a certificate of destruction.

A legal hold issued by the General Counsel suspends deletion of any records relevant to litigation, a regulatory inquiry, or an investigation until the General Counsel lifts it.

## 11. Sharing Personal Data

### 11.1 Processors and vendors

A vendor that processes personal data for the bank must sign a data processing agreement before receiving any data, and must pass the security assessment in VB-POL-001, section 11.

### 11.2 Regulators and law enforcement

Personal data is shared with regulators, the Financial Intelligence Bureau, courts, and law enforcement only when the request is in writing and approved by the General Counsel, or when the bank has a legal duty to report, such as suspicious transaction reports filed by the MLRO. Staff must never confirm customer information to a caller or visitor who claims to be from the police or a regulator. They must refer the person to the Legal Department.

### 11.3 Open banking and API partners

- Customer account data is shared with third-party providers through the bank's APIs only with the customer's explicit consent, given and recorded in Veridane Mobile or Veridane Online.
- Each consent lasts a maximum of 12 months, after which the customer must renew it. Customers can view and revoke their consents at any time in the app.
- The developer sandbox contains synthetic test data only. Real customer data must never be used in the sandbox or in any development or test environment.

### 11.4 International transfers

Personal data may be transferred or stored outside the country only with approval from both the DPO and the CISO, and only under a contract that gives the data equivalent protection.

## 12. Privacy by Design and Impact Assessments

A data protection impact assessment (DPIA) must be completed and signed off by the DPO before the bank launches any new product, channel, system, or vendor relationship that:

- processes personal data of more than 10,000 people;
- uses sensitive personal data, including biometrics;
- uses automated decision-making, such as credit scoring or AI-based fraud decisions;
- tracks location or device behavior.

DFS must complete a DPIA before launching any new digital channel or significant app feature.

## 13. Personal Data Breaches

- Staff report any suspected breach to the SOC within one hour of discovery, as required by VB-POL-001.
- The SOC informs the DPO immediately, and the DPO assesses the risk to the people affected.
- If the breach is likely to put people's rights at risk, the DPO notifies the DPC within 72 hours of the bank becoming aware of it.
- If the risk to affected people is high, the bank informs them without undue delay and tells them what they can do to protect themselves.
- Every breach is recorded in the breach register, whether or not it is notified.

## 14. Training

All staff complete the annual data protection course by 30 September each year. New staff complete it within 30 days of their start date.

## 15. Compliance and Enforcement

Breaches of this policy are handled under the disciplinary procedure in VB-POL-004. Accessing customer data without a business reason is gross misconduct.
