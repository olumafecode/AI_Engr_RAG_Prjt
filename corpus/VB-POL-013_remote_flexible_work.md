# Remote and Flexible Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out when staff may work away from bank premises, how flexible working hours work, and the security rules that apply to remote work. Its aim is to allow flexibility without weakening customer service or security.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff members. Contractors may work remotely only if their contract allows it and the CISO has approved their remote access.

## 3. Definitions

- **Remote work:** work performed away from bank premises, usually at home.
- **Hybrid arrangement:** an approved pattern of regular remote days each week.
- **Enrolled personal device:** a staff member's own phone or tablet registered in the bank's mobile device management (MDM) system so it can be used for bank email and collaboration apps.
- **Selective wipe:** removal of bank apps and data from an enrolled personal device without touching the owner's personal data.

## 4. Eligibility

### 4.1 Hybrid work

| Group | Remote days allowed | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Head office and regional office staff in eligible roles | Up to 2 days per week | Line manager |
| Technology Division and DFS product and engineering teams | Up to 3 days per week | Line manager, plus the CIO/CTO or the CDO |

### 4.2 Roles that are not eligible

These roles must be performed on bank premises and are not eligible for regular remote work:

- all branch-based roles, including BMs, BOMs, tellers, and CSOs;
- vault custodians and Cash Center staff;
- Treasury dealers, who must trade from the dealing room on recorded lines;
- DFS Contact Center, Fraud Desk, and SOC staff, except under section 4.4.

### 4.3 Other conditions

- Staff in their probation period (the first 6 months) are not eligible.
- Staff on a final written warning (VB-POL-004) or a Performance Improvement Plan (VB-POL-010) lose eligibility until it ends.
- Fully remote working is allowed only in exceptional cases, with the CHRO's approval, for no more than 3 months at a time.

### 4.4 Business continuity

If offices cannot be used, for example during a declared business continuity event, the COO and the CRO may direct any staff member with a bank-issued laptop, including DFS Contact Center, Fraud Desk, and SOC staff, to work remotely until the event ends.

## 5. Applying for a Hybrid Arrangement

- Staff apply through the HR portal and sign a Remote Work Agreement that states their remote days and work location.
- Before the first remote day, the staff member completes the home workspace self-assessment in the HR portal.
- Teams agree at least one shared office day each week.
- The line manager reviews each arrangement every year.
- The line manager may change or end an arrangement with 5 business days' notice, or immediately if there is a security or performance concern.

## 6. Work Location

- Remote work is normally done at the home address registered with HR. Working from another location in the country requires the line manager's approval.
- Working from abroad is limited to 10 business days per calendar year. It requires approval from the line manager and the CISO, requested at least 10 business days in advance, and only the bank laptop may be used. Remote work is never allowed from a sanctioned country or from a high-risk country identified by the CCO (VB-POL-007).
- Staff must not work in public places such as cafes or public transport, where screens can be seen or calls overheard, except to read Public or Internal information.

## 7. Working Hours and Availability

- Remote staff keep the normal working hours of their office (VB-ORG-001, section 6).
- Core hours are 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. During core hours, staff must be reachable by phone and collaboration tools.
- With the line manager's agreement, head office and regional office staff may start at any time between 7:00 a.m. and 9:30 a.m. and finish after a full working day. This applies on office days and remote days.
- Part-time and job-share arrangements require the CHRO's approval.
- Branch staff may swap shifts with a colleague with the BM's approval, provided minimum staffing is maintained.
- Remote work is not a substitute for leave. Staff who are unwell take sick leave (VB-POL-009) instead of working remotely, and staff on any kind of leave, including mandatory block leave, do not work.

## 8. Security Requirements

### 8.1 Devices

- Remote work is done on a bank-issued laptop.
- Personal phones and tablets may be enrolled in MDM for bank email, calendar, and collaboration apps only.
- Personal devices must never be used to access CoreLink, payment systems, or any Restricted information.
- Personal computers, whether desktop or laptop, must not be used for bank work.
- Family members and other people must not use bank devices.

### 8.2 Requirements for enrolled personal devices

- The operating system is a supported version, and security updates are installed within the time limits in VB-POL-001, section 7.
- The device has a passcode of at least 6 digits. Biometric unlock is allowed if it is backed by the passcode.
- Device encryption is switched on.
- The device is not jailbroken or rooted.
- Bank data is kept in a separate managed work profile.
- The staff member agrees that the bank may selectively wipe bank data if the device is lost, if the staff member leaves the bank (VB-POL-010), or if there is a security concern.

### 8.3 Network

- Staff connect to bank systems only through the bank VPN with multi-factor authentication (VB-POL-002, section 5.1).
- Home Wi-Fi must use WPA2 or stronger encryption, and the router's default password must be changed.
- Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected. Shared or public computers must never be used (VB-POL-001, section 8).

### 8.4 Handling information at home

- Screens lock after 5 minutes of inactivity (VB-POL-001). Staff lock their screen manually whenever they step away.
- Confidential and Restricted information must not be printed at home. Paper copies of Restricted information never leave bank premises (VB-POL-001, section 4).
- Calls involving customer or Confidential information take place in a private space where they cannot be overheard. Smart speakers and voice assistants must be switched off or out of earshot during these calls.
- Bank documents must never be saved to personal storage, personal email, or personal cloud accounts (VB-POL-001, section 5.2).

### 8.5 Lost devices and incidents

A lost or stolen bank laptop or enrolled device, and any other security incident, must be reported to the SOC within one hour (VB-POL-001, section 12).

## 9. Equipment, Allowance, and Health and Safety

- The bank provides a laptop and headset. Staff with an approved hybrid arrangement may also request a monitor and keyboard. Equipment staff buy themselves is not reimbursed unless the line manager approved it in advance.
- Staff with an approved hybrid arrangement receive a remote work allowance of USD 30 per month through payroll, to cover home internet and utility costs. These costs cannot be claimed as expenses (VB-POL-012).
- Work-related injuries at home must be reported to HR within 2 business days.
- Bank equipment is returned when the arrangement ends or the staff member leaves the bank.

## 10. Performance and Conduct

Remote staff are held to the same performance standards and the same Ethics and Code of Conduct Policy (VB-POL-004) as office-based staff. Managers assess remote staff on the results they deliver.

## 11. Leaving the Bank

On the last working day, bank equipment is returned and bank data is removed from enrolled personal devices by selective wipe (VB-POL-010, section 13.5).

## 12. Compliance and Enforcement

A breach of the security requirements in section 8 may lead to immediate withdrawal of remote work eligibility and to disciplinary action under VB-POL-004.
