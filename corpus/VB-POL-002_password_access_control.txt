VERIDANE BANK
PASSWORD AND ACCESS CONTROL POLICY

Document ID: VB-POL-002
Version: 3.1
Effective Date: 2026-02-01
Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
Approved By: Board Risk Committee
Review Cycle: Annual
Classification: Internal


1. PURPOSE

This policy sets the rules for passwords, multi-factor authentication, and the granting, review, and removal of access to Veridane Bank systems. It supports the Information Security Policy (VB-POL-001).


2. SCOPE

This policy applies to every staff member, contractor, and vendor who holds an account on any bank system. It covers standard user accounts, privileged accounts, and service accounts on all bank systems, including CoreLink, the bank's core banking system.


3. DEFINITIONS

Standard account: an account used by one person for everyday work.

Privileged account: an account with administrative rights on a system, database, or network device, or the ability to change security settings or other users' access.

Service account: a non-human account used by an application or scheduled process.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA): sign-in that requires something you know (a password) plus something you have (an enrolled phone or a hardware token).

PAM vault: the bank's privileged access management system. It stores privileged credentials, issues them for limited periods, and records their use.


4. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Minimum requirements by account type

Standard accounts
- Minimum length: 14 characters
- Change: every 180 days, and immediately if compromise is suspected
- History: the last 12 passwords cannot be reused

Privileged accounts
- Minimum length: 16 characters
- Change: every 60 days, and automatically after each PAM vault checkout
- History: the last 24 passwords cannot be reused

Service accounts
- Minimum length: 25 characters, generated by the PAM vault
- Change: every 12 months, and within 5 business days after anyone who knew the password leaves the bank or changes role

4.2 Content rules

A password must not contain the user's username, first name, or last name, the word "Veridane", or the current year. Every new password is checked automatically against a list of known breached passwords and is rejected if it appears there.

4.3 Passphrases

Staff are encouraged to use a passphrase of four or more unrelated words. A passphrase is easier to remember and harder to guess than a short complex password.

4.4 Initial and reset passwords

Passwords issued by the IT Service Desk for a new account or a reset are one-time passwords. They must be changed at first sign-in and expire 72 hours after issue if not used. The password is always sent through a different channel from the username.

4.5 Account lockout

An account locks for 30 minutes after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts. The IT Service Desk may unlock an account or reset a password only after verifying the staff member's identity by calling them back on the phone number held in the HR system. The Service Desk will never reset a password based on an email request alone.

4.6 Session timeouts

CoreLink sessions end after 10 minutes of inactivity. Other business applications end sessions after 15 minutes of inactivity. These timeouts are separate from the 5-minute screen lock required by VB-POL-001.


5. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION

5.1 Where MFA is required

- VPN and all other remote access
- All privileged accounts
- Email and collaboration tools when used outside the bank network
- CoreLink supervisor overrides and payment authorizations
- Any cloud service that holds Confidential or Restricted information

5.2 Approved methods

The approved MFA methods are the Veridane Authenticator app on an enrolled phone and a bank-issued hardware token. Codes sent by SMS are allowed only as a temporary fallback, for no more than 7 days, while a replacement phone or token is issued.

5.3 Unexpected prompts

Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start. Report any unexpected prompt to the SOC (ext. 4911) straight away, because it usually means someone else has your password.


6. STORING AND SHARING CREDENTIALS

6.1 Passwords and MFA codes must never be shared with anyone, including colleagues, line managers, and IT staff. IT staff will never ask for them.

6.2 Passwords must not be written down or stored in plain-text files, spreadsheets, email, or browsers on shared devices. The approved tool for storing passwords is Veridane Password Safe.

6.3 Shared or generic user accounts are not allowed. The only exception is system accounts held in the PAM vault, where each use is checked out by a named person.

6.4 Tellers must never allow another person to work on their CoreLink session. A teller must log out or lock the terminal whenever leaving the counter, even briefly (see VB-POL-008).

6.5 A staff member who suspects someone else knows their password must change it immediately and report it to the SOC within one hour.


7. REQUESTING AND GRANTING ACCESS

7.1 All access requests are made through the Access Request Form in the Veridane Service Portal.

7.2 Every request needs approval from the line manager and the system owner. Requests for Restricted systems and for any privileged access also need CISO approval.

7.3 IT provisions approved access within 2 business days of final approval.

7.4 Each department has role-based access profiles. Access beyond the standard profile for a role requires a written business justification.

7.5 Before access is granted, it is checked against the segregation of duties conflict matrix maintained under the Internal Control Policy (VB-POL-006). Conflicting combinations are blocked. Examples include initiating and authorizing the same payment, and creating a customer record and approving that customer's account opening. The CRO may approve a conflicting combination only where a documented compensating control is in place.


8. ACCESS REVIEWS

8.1 Review frequency

- Privileged accounts: every quarter
- CoreLink and payment systems: every quarter
- All other systems: every 6 months

8.2 Line managers and system owners must complete each review within 15 business days of its launch. Access not confirmed by the deadline is suspended.

8.3 Internal Audit tests a sample of review results every year.


9. JOINERS, MOVERS, AND LEAVERS

9.1 Joiners: no access is granted before the staff member's start date or before they have signed the acceptable use acknowledgment.

9.2 Movers: access from a previous role is removed within 5 business days of the transfer date. Access for the new role must be requested through the normal process.

9.3 Leavers: HR notifies IT at least 5 business days before a staff member's last working day, and all access is disabled by the end of that day. Where a staff member is summarily dismissed or escorted from the premises, all access is disabled within 4 hours of HR's notification (see VB-POL-010).

9.4 Disabled accounts are deleted after 90 days. Mailbox content is kept according to the records retention schedule in VB-POL-003.

9.5 Dormant accounts: an account unused for 45 consecutive days is disabled automatically. Re-enabling it requires line manager approval.


10. PRIVILEGED ACCESS

10.1 Administrators use a separate privileged account for administrative work. Privileged accounts must never be used for email or web browsing.

10.2 Privileged credentials are checked out from the PAM vault for no more than 8 hours at a time. Sessions are recorded, and the password changes automatically when the credential is checked back in.

10.3 Emergency ("break-glass") accounts are sealed in the PAM vault. Using one requires approval from the CISO, or from the CRO if the CISO is unavailable. Each use is reviewed within 1 business day.


11. ACCESS DURING MANDATORY LEAVE

Staff in sensitive roles who take mandatory block leave under the Leave and Public Holidays Policy (VB-POL-009) and the Internal Control Policy (VB-POL-006) have their CoreLink and payment system access suspended for the whole leave period. Access is restored on their first working day back, after the line manager confirms their return.


12. COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

Breaches of this policy are handled under the disciplinary procedure in the Ethics and Code of Conduct Policy (VB-POL-004). Knowingly sharing a password or MFA code, or using another person's credentials, is treated as gross misconduct.


13. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions follow the Policy Exception Request process in VB-ORG-001, section 8.3.
