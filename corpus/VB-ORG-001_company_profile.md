# Company Profile and Policy Framework

*Veridane Bank is a fictional organization created for an academic project. All people, places, regulators, laws, and systems named in this document set are fictional.*

## 1. About Veridane Bank

Veridane Bank is a commercial bank headquartered at 14 Admiralty Crescent, Port Halden. It was founded in 1998 as Veridane Savings and Loans and received a full commercial banking license from the Central Monetary Authority in 2009. As of January 2026 the bank operates 62 branches across four regions, employs about 3,150 staff, and serves roughly 1.2 million customers.

The bank has five business segments:

- **Retail Banking:** current and savings accounts, personal loans, and mortgages for individual customers.
- **SME Banking:** accounts, working capital, and trade services for businesses with annual turnover below USD 10 million.
- **Corporate and Commercial Banking:** lending, cash management, and trade finance for larger businesses and public sector bodies.
- **Treasury and Markets:** liquidity management, foreign exchange, and investment of the bank's own funds.
- **Digital Financial Services (DFS):** all of the bank's channels and digital touchpoints inside and outside branches, including ATMs, digital hubs, online banking, mobile banking, debit and credit cards, POS terminals, wallets, and API and sandbox services. DFS serves customers of every other segment.

All monetary amounts in Veridane policies are stated in US dollars (USD).

### 1.2 Digital Financial Services

DFS is led by the Chief Digital Officer (CDO), who reports directly to the CEO. Five business heads report to the CDO:

| DFS unit | Led by | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Banking | Head, Digital Banking | Veridane Online (internet banking) and Veridane Mobile (mobile banking app) |
| Cards | Head, Cards | Debit, credit, and prepaid cards, including issuance, limits, and card disputes |
| Merchant Acquiring and Payments | Head, Merchant Acquiring and Payments | POS terminals, merchant onboarding, and the e-commerce payment gateway |
| Self-Service Channels | Head, Self-Service Channels | ATMs, cash deposit machines, and digital hubs |
| Wallets and Open Banking | Head, Wallets and Open Banking | Veridane Wallet, API services for partners, and the developer sandbox |

As of January 2026 the bank operates 410 ATMs, of which 180 accept cash deposits, 18 digital hubs, and about 9,500 POS terminals at merchant locations. Digital hubs are self-service lounges with ATMs, cash deposit machines, account-opening kiosks, and video banking with contact center agents. Twelve hubs are inside branches and six are in shopping malls and on university campuses.

DFS also owns any new channel or digital touchpoint the bank introduces, unless EXCO assigns it elsewhere.

### 1.3 Technology

The Chief Information Officer / Chief Technology Officer (CIO/CTO) leads the Technology Division and reports to the CEO. The CIO/CTO is responsible for the bank's technology infrastructure, including CoreLink (the core banking system), the data centers (primary site in Port Halden, disaster recovery site in Saltmere), networks, end-user devices, IT Operations, and the IT Service Desk.

The Technology Division supports the operational objectives of four areas: branch operations, Treasury Operations, DFS, and head office (back office) operations.

To keep security oversight independent of technology delivery, the CISO reports to the CRO rather than to the CIO/CTO.

## 2. Regulators and External Bodies

- **Central Monetary Authority (CMA):** licenses and supervises the bank. Receives regulatory returns and notifications of major operational and security incidents.
- **Data Protection Commission (DPC):** supervises the handling of personal data and receives personal data breach notifications.
- **Financial Intelligence Bureau (FIB):** receives suspicious transaction reports and currency transaction reports.

## 3. Governance

The Board of Directors sets strategy and risk appetite and approves all bank-wide policies. Three Board committees support it:

- **Board Audit Committee (BAC):** oversees financial reporting, internal audit, and external audit.
- **Board Risk Committee (BRC):** oversees enterprise risk, compliance, information security, and the policy exceptions register.
- **Board Nominations and Remuneration Committee (BNRC):** oversees senior appointments, pay, and succession.

The Executive Committee (EXCO), chaired by the CEO, runs the bank day to day and meets every Tuesday.

The bank follows a three lines of defense model. Business units and branches own and manage their risks (first line). Risk Management and Compliance set frameworks and monitor them (second line). Internal Audit provides independent assurance to the Board Audit Committee (third line).

## 6. Working Hours

- **Head office and regional offices:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Branch banking halls:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Nine designated Saturday branches also open from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.
- **Branch staff** report by 7:30 a.m. so opening procedures are complete before customers arrive (see VB-POL-008).
- **Digital hubs:** 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. daily.
- **Digital channels** (Veridane Mobile, Veridane Online, Veridane Wallet, cards, ATMs, and POS) are available 24/7. Veridane Online and Veridane Mobile may be unavailable during planned maintenance on Sundays from 1:00 a.m. to 3:00 a.m. Card, ATM, and POS services stay available during this window.

A **business day** is any Monday to Friday that is not a public holiday listed in the Leave and Public Holidays Policy (VB-POL-009).

## 8. Policy Framework

### 8.1 Document hierarchy

1. **Policy:** states what the bank requires and why. Approved by the Board or a Board committee.
2. **Standard:** sets mandatory technical or operational specifics that support a policy. Approved by EXCO.
3. **Procedure:** gives step-by-step instructions for carrying out a standard. Approved by the relevant Head of Department.
4. **Guideline:** recommended practice. Not mandatory.

Where documents conflict, applicable law and regulation take precedence, then policies, then standards, then procedures. Where two policies conflict, the more restrictive requirement applies until the policy owners resolve the conflict.

### 8.2 Ownership, approval, and review

Every policy has a named owner at Band B or above. The Company Secretary keeps the Policy Register and publishes current versions on the staff intranet. Each policy is reviewed at least once a year, and within 60 days of a material regulatory change or a Severity 1 incident related to the policy's subject.

### 8.3 Policy exceptions

A staff member who cannot meet a policy requirement must submit a Policy Exception Request (PER) through the Veridane Service Portal before acting outside the policy. Exceptions:

- are approved jointly by the policy owner and the CRO;
- last no longer than 6 months and may be renewed once;
- must record the risk, the compensating controls, and an end date;
- can never permit anything that breaches law or regulation.

Risk Management keeps an exceptions register, which the Board Risk Committee reviews every quarter.

### 8.4 Annual attestation

By 31 March each year, all staff must confirm through the HR portal that they have read and will comply with the Ethics and Code of Conduct Policy, the Information Security Policy, and the Data Protection and Privacy Policy. New staff complete the attestation within 10 business days of their start date.

### 8.5 Breaches

Breaches of any policy are handled under the disciplinary procedure in the Ethics and Code of Conduct Policy (VB-POL-004).

## 10. Definitions

- **Staff member:** any permanent, fixed-term, or temporary employee of the bank, including interns.
- **Contractor:** an individual engaged through a third party who works on bank premises or bank systems.
- **Line manager:** the person a staff member reports to directly.
- **Business day:** see section 6.
