1. Purpose

This policy sets the operating standards for every Veridane Bank branch. It covers opening and closing, cash handling, counter services, customer service, security, and the ATMs and digital hubs located in branches. Its aim is a branch network that is safe for customers and staff and controlled consistently across all four regions.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all 62 branches, including the nine designated Saturday branches, and to the 12 digital hubs located inside branches. It applies to all branch staff, regional staff who work in branches, and contractors such as security guards and cash-in-transit crews while they are on branch premises.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

  • Head of Branch Operations (HBO): owns this policy, assigns each branch a category, and sets vault cash limits.
  • Regional Manager (RM): oversees the branches in a region and approves emergency branch closures.
  • Branch Manager (BM): is accountable for everything that happens in the branch, including physical security of the branch ATMs and in-branch digital hub.
  • Branch Operations Manager (BOM): runs daily operations, serves as a vault custodian, and verifies teller balances.
  • Tellers: handle cash transactions within their authorization limits.
  • Customer Service Officers (CSOs): open accounts, handle enquiries, and log complaints.
  • DFS Self-Service Channels: owns ATMs, cash deposit machines, and digital hub equipment, and is responsible for keeping them working.

4. Branch Hours

  • Banking halls open Monday to Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. The nine designated Saturday branches also open from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.
  • Branch staff report by 7:30 a.m.
  • The doors close at 4:00 p.m. Customers already inside the banking hall at that time are served.
  • Branches are closed on the public holidays listed in VB-POL-009.
  • A branch may close during business hours only in an emergency and with the RM's approval. Corporate Communications notifies customers through a branch notice and a Veridane Mobile notification.

5. Opening Procedures

  1. Two staff members, one of whom is the BM or the BOM, form the opening pair and arrive by 7:15 a.m.
  2. The opening pair inspect the outside of the branch before entering. If they see signs of forced entry or anything suspicious, they must not enter. They call the Bank Security Control Room (ext. 4600, available 24/7) and wait for instructions.
  3. The opening pair enter, disarm the alarm, and check the premises. Other staff are let in only after the opening pair give the all-clear.
  4. The vault time lock releases at 7:45 a.m. The vault is then opened under dual control by two custodians.
  5. The BOM issues opening cash to each teller against a teller cash receipt recorded in CoreLink.
  6. Staff check that CCTV, panic alarms, ATMs, and the digital hub are working. ATM and digital hub faults are reported to the DFS Staff Helpdesk (ext. 4300).
  7. The BOM completes the opening checklist in CoreLink by 7:55 a.m., and the doors open at 8:00 a.m.

6. Cash Management

6.1 Vault cash limits

The HBO assigns every branch to a category each year based on its transaction volume. Cash above the vault limit must be sent to the Cash Center the same day through the approved cash-in-transit vendor.

Branch categoryVault cash limit (USD)
A (high volume)250,000
B (medium volume)150,000
C (low volume)80,000

6.2 Teller cash limit

A teller may hold no more than USD 20,000 at the counter. Cash above this limit is transferred to the vault during the day.

6.3 Vault custodians

The vault has two custodians: the BOM and one senior officer appointed in writing by the BM. Each custodian holds one half of the combination and must never share it. The combination is changed every 6 months and whenever a custodian changes. Custodians are subject to mandatory block leave and job rotation under VB-POL-006.

6.4 Surprise cash counts

The BM counts each teller's cash without notice at least once a month and counts the vault at least once a quarter, as required by VB-POL-006.

6.5 Cash differences

  • A teller who finds a shortage or overage must report it to the BOM immediately. The difference is recorded in the CoreLink cash difference register.
  • Overages are credited to the branch suspense account. A teller must never keep an overage.
  • The BOM investigates any shortage above USD 100 and reports the findings to the BM within 1 business day.
  • A teller with three shortages in any 3-month period is referred to HR under VB-POL-004.

6.6 Counterfeit notes

A suspected counterfeit note is never returned to the person who presented it. The teller keeps the note, gives the customer a receipt, and records it in the counterfeit register. The branch sends the note to the Cash Center within 2 business days, and the Cash Center forwards it to the CMA.

6.7 Cash in transit

Cash is collected and delivered only by the bank's approved cash-in-transit vendor. Branch staff verify each crew's identity cards and pre-advised code before handing over or accepting cash. Collection times are varied so that no fixed pattern forms.

7. Counter Services

7.1 Account opening

  • Accounts are opened under the Know Your Customer and Know Your Customer's Business Policy (VB-POL-007).
  • CSOs must see original documents, stamp each copy "Original Sighted", and capture the customer's fingerprint.
  • An account is activated only after the Central KYC Unit approves the KYC record.
  • High-risk accounts need approval from the BM and the MLRO before activation.

7.2 Cash deposits

  • Cash deposits of USD 5,000 or more in one business day need a signed source of funds declaration from the depositor.
  • CoreLink automatically produces a Currency Transaction Report when a customer's cash transactions total USD 10,000 or more in one business day.
  • Staff who notice deposits that seem to be split to stay below these amounts must submit an Internal Suspicious Activity Report to the MLRO (VB-POL-007, section 14).

7.3 Cash withdrawals

  • The teller verifies the customer's ID and signature before paying out.
  • Authorization limits: the teller alone up to USD 5,000; the BOM from USD 5,001 to USD 50,000; the BM above USD 50,000 (VB-POL-006).
  • Withdrawals of USD 20,000 or more need 1 business day's notice so the branch can arrange the cash.
  • A third party may withdraw cash for an account holder only with a written mandate signed by the account holder and the third party's original ID. Third-party withdrawals are limited to USD 5,000 per day.

7.4 Teller conduct

  • Tellers must never work on another teller's CoreLink session. They lock or log out of their terminal and lock their cash drawer whenever they leave the counter, even briefly (VB-POL-002).
  • Personal mobile phones are kept in staff lockers during counter duty.
  • Tellers must not process transactions on their own accounts or those of relatives or close associates (VB-POL-004).
  • Staff must never ask a customer for their card PIN, a one-time passcode, or an online banking password.

8. Customer Service and Complaints

8.1 Service standards

  • Customers should be attended to within 15 minutes of arrival. When waiting times exceed 15 minutes, the BOM opens another counter or directs customers with routine transactions to the digital hub.
  • Elderly customers aged 65 and over, pregnant women, and customers with disabilities receive priority service.

8.2 Complaints

9. Clean Desk and Counter

  • Customer documents must not be left on counters or where other customers can see them.
  • Teller and CSO screens are positioned so that customers in the banking hall cannot read them.
  • All Confidential and Restricted documents are locked away at the end of each day, in line with VB-POL-001.

10. ATMs and Digital Hubs

  • DFS Self-Service Channels owns ATMs, cash deposit machines, and digital hub equipment. The BM is responsible for their physical security and for a daily visual check for damage or skimming devices.
  • If staff suspect a skimming device, they take the ATM out of service, leave the device in place, and call the Fraud Desk (staff ext. 4777) and the Bank Security Control Room.
  • ATMs are replenished under dual control, and ATM cash is reconciled daily (VB-POL-006).
  • Captured cards are removed under dual control, recorded in the captured card register, and kept in the vault for 5 business days. A card is returned only to the cardholder, on presentation of original ID. Unclaimed cards are destroyed under dual control on the sixth business day, and the customer is notified by SMS.
  • In-branch digital hubs have their own entrance and are physically separated from the banking hall and back office, so they can stay open from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. after the banking hall closes.

11. Branch Security

11.1 CCTV and alarms

  • CCTV records 24 hours a day. Footage is kept for 90 days (VB-POL-003). Footage is released only with the DPO's approval, or to law enforcement with the General Counsel's approval.
  • Panic buttons are fitted at every teller station and in the BM's office. They are tested every month with the Bank Security Control Room.

11.2 Access to the back office

  • The back office is entered only with a staff access card. Customers are never allowed into the back office.
  • Visitors and contractors sign the visitor log and are escorted at all times.

11.3 Robbery

  1. Comply with the robbers' demands and do not resist. Activate the silent alarm only if it is safe to do so.
  2. Note descriptions of the robbers, their weapons, and their vehicle, and do not touch anything they handled.
  3. When the robbers have left, lock the doors, call the Bank Security Control Room and the police, and close the branch.
  4. Staff must not speak to the media. All media enquiries go to Corporate Communications (VB-POL-011).
  5. HR offers counseling to every staff member present.
  6. The BM records the loss in the operational loss database, as required by VB-POL-006.

11.4 Fire and evacuation

Every branch holds a fire evacuation drill twice a year. During an evacuation, staff secure cash only if it is safe to do so and gather at the branch's designated assembly point.

12. End-of-Day and Closing Procedures

  1. Tellers balance their cash by 4:45 p.m. The BOM verifies each teller's balance and signs it off in CoreLink.
  2. All cash is returned to the vault under dual control. The vault is closed and the time lock set by 5:30 p.m.
  3. The BOM completes the end-of-day checklist in CoreLink, including clearing items in branch suspense accounts.
  4. Confidential documents are locked away and workstations are shut down.
  5. The closing pair, one of whom is the BM or the BOM, are the last to leave. They check the premises and set the alarm. All staff leave by 6:00 p.m.
  6. Working after 6:00 p.m. requires the BM's approval and notice to the Bank Security Control Room. No one may work alone in a branch at any time.

13. Saturday Branches

  • Nine designated branches open on Saturdays from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.
  • Minimum staffing is one BOM or designated officer, two tellers, and one security guard.
  • Saturday services are cash deposits, cash withdrawals up to USD 5,000, card collection, balance enquiries and statements, and logging complaints. Accounts are not opened on Saturdays.
  • Staff who work on a Saturday receive a day off in lieu, to be taken within the following 2 weeks.

14. Monitoring

  • Regional Internal Control Officers carry out unannounced checks of every branch at least once a quarter (VB-POL-006).
  • Each branch receives a monthly scorecard, reviewed by the RM, covering cash differences, complaint resolution times, waiting times, and open audit issues.

15. Compliance and Enforcement

Breaches of this policy are handled under the disciplinary procedure in VB-POL-004. Breaking dual control rules or leaving a CoreLink session open for another person to use may be treated as gross misconduct.
