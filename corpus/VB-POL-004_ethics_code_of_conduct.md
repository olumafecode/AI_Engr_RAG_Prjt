# Ethics and Code of Conduct Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the standards of behavior Veridane Bank expects from everyone who works for it. It also contains the bank's disciplinary procedure, which applies to breaches of any bank policy.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff members, including interns and fixed-term staff, and to members of the Board. Contractors and vendors working on bank premises or systems must follow sections 5 to 9 while working for the bank.

## 3. Our Values

- **Integrity:** we are honest, and we do the right thing when no one is watching.
- **Customer first:** we treat customers fairly and explain our products clearly.
- **Accountability:** we own our decisions and our mistakes.
- **Respect:** we treat colleagues, customers, and communities with dignity.
- **Prudence:** we protect the bank's money, and our customers' money, as if it were our own.

## 4. General Standards

Every staff member must:

- comply with the law, regulations, and bank policies;
- act honestly and avoid conduct that could damage the bank's reputation;
- treat customers fairly, give accurate information, and never mis-sell products;
- keep accurate records and never falsify, alter, or back-date documents or system entries;
- cooperate fully with internal and external audits, investigations, and regulators.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest

5.1 A conflict of interest arises when a personal interest, relationship, or activity could influence, or appear to influence, a staff member's decisions at work.

5.2 Staff must declare any actual or potential conflict in the Conflicts Register on the HR portal within 5 business days of it arising. All staff also confirm their declarations each year as part of the annual attestation due by 31 March (VB-ORG-001, section 8.4).

5.3 Staff must not:

- process, approve, or authorize any transaction, loan, fee waiver, or account change on their own account or on the account of a relative or close personal associate;
- take part in any decision to hire, promote, or supervise a relative;
- take part in selecting or managing a vendor in which they or a relative have a financial interest.

5.4 Outside employment, paid consultancy, and directorships of other companies require written approval from the line manager and the CCO before they begin. Band A staff also need approval from the Chair of the Board Nominations and Remuneration Committee.

## 6. Gifts and Hospitality

6.1 Staff may accept modest gifts and hospitality that are infrequent and could not reasonably be seen as influencing a business decision. All gifts and hospitality offered, whether accepted or declined, must be recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality Register within 5 business days.

6.2 Value limits per gift:

| Value (USD) | Rule |
|---|---|
| Up to 50 | May be accepted. Record in the register. |
| 51 to 250 | Line manager approval required before accepting. |
| Above 250 | Must be declined, or handed to Compliance if declining would cause serious offense. |

6.3 Staff must never accept cash or cash equivalents, including gift cards, vouchers, wallet transfers, or airtime, whatever the value.

6.4 Staff involved in a procurement tender must not accept any gift or hospitality from any bidder while the tender is open.

6.5 Travel or accommodation offered by a vendor or customer requires the CCO's written approval before it is accepted.

6.6 Gifts or hospitality given to public officials, including regulators, require the CCO's written approval in advance.

## 7. Anti-Bribery and Corruption

The bank has zero tolerance for bribery. Staff must never offer, give, request, or accept anything of value to improperly influence a decision. Facilitation payments, meaning small payments to speed up routine government actions, are prohibited. Any request for a bribe must be reported to Compliance within one business day.

## 8. Personal Financial Conduct

- Staff must manage their personal finances responsibly.
- Staff must not borrow money from, or lend money to, customers, and must not act as guarantor for a customer's facility.
- Staff in Treasury and Markets must pre-clear personal securities and foreign exchange trades with Compliance and hold any investment for at least 30 days.
- Staff must not use information gained at work to trade, or advise others to trade.

## 9. Confidentiality

Staff must keep customer and bank information confidential, in line with VB-POL-001 and VB-POL-003. This duty continues after a staff member leaves the bank. Staff must never discuss a customer's affairs outside work, including with family, and must not look up any account without a business reason.

## 10. Respect in the Workplace

10.1 The bank does not tolerate harassment, bullying, or discrimination on any grounds, including sex, age, disability, religion, ethnicity, or marital status.

10.2 Sexual harassment, including unwelcome comments, messages, or physical contact, is gross misconduct.

10.3 Staff who experience or witness this behavior may report it to their line manager, to the HR Service Center (ext. 4200), or through the Speak Up Line (VB-POL-005).

## 11. Public Statements and Social Media

Only authorized spokespersons may speak for the bank to the media. Staff must not disclose confidential information or comment on customers, colleagues, or bank matters on social media. Detailed rules are in the Internal and Corporate Communication Policy (VB-POL-011).

## 12. Dress and Appearance

Branch staff wear the official Veridane uniform from Monday to Thursday and business casual on Fridays and Saturdays. Head office staff wear business attire from Monday to Thursday and business casual on Fridays. Staff attending external meetings wear business attire whatever the day.

## 13. Alcohol and Drugs

Staff must not come to work, or work, under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs. Alcohol may be served at official bank events only with the approval of a Band A executive.

## 14. Political Activity

Staff may take part in political activity in their own time. Bank premises, systems, and resources must not be used for political activity, and staff must not imply that the bank supports any party or candidate. A staff member who intends to stand for public office must inform the CHRO in writing before being nominated.

## 15. Raising Concerns

Staff who suspect a breach of this policy or of the law should raise it with their line manager, with Compliance, or through the Speak Up Line. Suspected fraud on a customer account, card, or wallet can also be reported to the Fraud Desk. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is gross misconduct (see VB-POL-005).

## 16. Disciplinary Procedure

This procedure applies to breaches of this policy and of any other bank policy.

### 16.1 Categories of misconduct

| Category | Examples | Usual outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Minor misconduct | Lateness, dress code breaches, late completion of mandatory training | Verbal warning, kept on file for 6 months |
| Misconduct | Repeated minor misconduct, careless handling of Confidential information, failing to declare a conflict of interest | Written warning, valid for 12 months |
| Serious misconduct | Misconduct while a written warning is still valid, negligence causing financial loss | Final written warning valid for 18 months, or demotion |
| Gross misconduct | Fraud, theft, bribery, falsifying records, sharing passwords or using another person's credentials, accessing customer data without a business reason, harassment, retaliation against a whistleblower | Summary dismissal (see VB-POL-010) |

### 16.2 Investigation

- HR investigates allegations of misconduct. Compliance or Internal Audit leads investigations involving fraud, bribery, or money laundering.
- A staff member may be suspended on full pay during an investigation for up to 30 days. The CHRO may extend a suspension once, by up to 30 more days.
- No one involved in the matter, or who reports to the person being investigated, may investigate it.

### 16.3 Hearing

- The staff member receives the allegations in writing at least 3 business days before the disciplinary hearing.
- The staff member may bring a colleague to the hearing.
- The decision is given in writing within 5 business days after the hearing.

### 16.4 Who decides

| Staff band | Decision-maker |
|---|---|
| C, D, E | Staff Disciplinary Committee, chaired by the CHRO |
| B | EXCO Disciplinary Panel, chaired by the CEO |
| A | Board Nominations and Remuneration Committee |

### 16.5 Appeals

A staff member may appeal within 10 business days of receiving the decision. Appeals for Bands C to E are heard by a panel chaired by the COO. Appeals for Band B go to the Chair of the BNRC, and appeals for Band A go to the Board Chair. The appeal decision is final.

## 17. Annual Attestation

All staff confirm by 31 March each year that they have read and will comply with this policy (VB-ORG-001, section 8.4).
