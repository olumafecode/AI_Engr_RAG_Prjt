# Expense and Business Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Veridane Bank pays for, how travel is approved and booked, what each staff band is entitled to, and how to claim reimbursement. It applies the bank's value of prudence: staff should spend the bank's money as carefully as their own.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff members who incur expenses or travel on bank business. Vendor invoices, including hotel and travel agency invoices billed directly to the bank, are paid through Accounts Payable under the authorization limits in the Internal Control Policy (VB-POL-006, section 6.3).

## 3. Principles

An expense is reimbursed only if it is:

- necessary for bank business;
- reasonable in cost and the most economical practical option;
- supported by a receipt where section 12 requires one;
- approved in line with this policy.

Staff must not gain personally from business travel or expenses.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

- **CFO:** owns this policy and approves exceptions to it.
- **Line managers:** approve travel requests and expense claims for their team and check that each expense is genuine and within policy.
- **Corporate Travel Desk:** part of Procurement. Books all flights and hotels for business travel.
- **Finance Expense Team:** checks and pays claims and audits a sample of them.
- **All staff:** follow this policy and submit accurate claims on time.

## 5. Travel Approval and Booking

### 5.1 Approval

Travel requests are submitted in the Veridane Expense Portal.

| Trip | Approval | Request submitted |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic day trip | Line manager | No minimum notice |
| Domestic travel with an overnight stay | Line manager | At least 5 business days before travel |
| International travel | Head of Department and the Band A executive of the division | At least 15 business days before travel |
| International travel by Band A staff | CEO | At least 15 business days before travel |

### 5.2 Booking

- All flights and hotels are booked through the Corporate Travel Desk. Bookings made personally are not reimbursed, unless the Travel Desk could not be reached in an emergency and the line manager approves.
- Staff may keep airline and hotel loyalty points, but loyalty programs must not influence the choice of airline or hotel.

### 5.3 High-risk destinations

Travel to a country the CCO has identified as high-risk (VB-POL-007) requires the CRO's approval and a security briefing before departure. Business travel to sanctioned countries is not allowed.

## 6. Air Travel

| Band | Domestic flights | International flights |
|---|---|---|
| A | Economy | Business class |
| B | Economy | Business class for flights over 6 hours; otherwise economy |
| C, D, E | Economy | Economy |

Flight time means the scheduled duration of a single flight, not the total journey time including connections. Staff may pay for an upgrade themselves. First-class travel is never reimbursed.

## 7. Ground Transport

- Taxis and ride-hailing services may be used on business trips, including to and from airports.
- Band A and Band B staff may use the bank's executive car service.
- For long road journeys between cities, staff should use a bank pool car with a bank driver where one is available.
- Staff who use their own car on bank business are reimbursed at USD 0.45 per kilometer for distance beyond their normal commute.
- Car rental requires line manager approval and is limited to the standard mid-size category.
- Traffic and parking fines are never reimbursed.

## 8. Accommodation

Maximum nightly room rate, excluding taxes:

| Band | Domestic (USD) | International (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| A | 250 | 400 |
| B | 180 | 300 |
| C | 140 | 220 |
| D and E | 110 | 180 |

- The Corporate Travel Desk books from the bank's approved hotel list.
- If a trip requires a hotel above the cap, for example because it is the conference venue, the Head of Department may approve the higher rate in advance.
- Staff who stay with friends or family instead of a hotel receive USD 30 per night.

## 9. Meals and Incidentals (Per Diem)

A daily per diem covers meals, tips, and small incidental costs. No receipts are needed for the per diem.

| Band | Domestic per day (USD) | International per day (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| A | 80 | 120 |
| B | 70 | 100 |
| C | 60 | 90 |
| D and E | 50 | 80 |

- A day trip of more than 8 hours away from the usual workplace earns 50% of the domestic per diem.
- The per diem is reduced by 30% for each meal provided free during the day, for example by a conference or as part of the hotel rate.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed except as part of approved client entertainment (section 10).

## 10. Client Entertainment and Gifts

- Band B and more senior staff may host business meals with customers or prospective customers, up to USD 75 per person. Band C staff need the Head of Department's approval in advance.
- Amounts above USD 75 per person require approval from a Band A executive in advance.
- Every entertainment claim lists the attendees, their organizations, and the business purpose.
- Entertaining or giving gifts to public officials, including regulators, requires the CCO's written approval in advance (VB-POL-004, section 6.6).
- Gifts to customers are limited to bank-branded items worth no more than USD 50 per recipient and are recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality Register (VB-POL-004).
- Team celebrations are limited to USD 25 per person, no more than twice a year per team, with the Head of Department's approval.

## 11. Corporate Cards

- Staff in Bands A to C who travel or entertain regularly may be issued a Veridane corporate card, provided by DFS Cards.
- Monthly limits: Band A USD 10,000; Band B USD 5,000; Band C USD 2,500.
- The card is for business expenses only. An accidental personal charge must be reported to the line manager and repaid within 5 business days.
- Cash withdrawals are allowed only when traveling abroad, up to USD 300 per trip.
- Cardholders reconcile each statement in the Veridane Expense Portal within 10 business days of the statement date. A card whose statement has not been reconciled after 30 days is suspended.
- A lost or stolen corporate card must be reported immediately to the Fraud Desk (0800 555 0177) or the DFS Contact Center, and to the line manager.

## 12. Claiming Expenses

### 12.1 Submission

- Claims are submitted in the Veridane Expense Portal within 30 days of the expense, or within 30 days of the end of a trip.
- Claims submitted more than 90 days after the expense are not reimbursed.
- Itemized receipts are required for every expense of USD 25 or more, except the per diem.

### 12.2 Approval

| Claim | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 2,000 | Line manager |
| Above USD 2,000 | Line manager and Head of Department |
| Any claim by a Band A staff member | CEO |
| Any claim by the CEO | Chair of the Board Audit Committee |

No one may approve their own claim, or a claim that includes an expense incurred for them, such as a meal they attended.

### 12.3 Payment and audit

- Approved claims are paid into the staff member's salary account within 10 business days of final approval.
- The Finance Expense Team audits a random 10% of claims each month, and every claim above USD 5,000.

## 13. Cash Advances

Where a corporate card is not available, staff may request a travel cash advance of up to 80% of the expected per diem for the trip. The advance must be accounted for within 10 business days after returning. Any amount still outstanding after 30 days is deducted from salary.

## 14. Special Situations

- **Recall from leave:** when a staff member is recalled from annual leave under VB-POL-009, section 4.5, the bank reimburses reasonable costs of returning. This includes changed bookings and non-refundable bookings that could not be used, with the Head of Department's approval.
- **Relocation:** staff transferred to a branch or office more than 100 km from their current workplace, including transfers under job rotation (VB-POL-006, section 6.6), receive a relocation allowance of 1 month's basic salary plus moving costs through an approved mover.
- **Training and conferences:** fees are paid from the HR learning budget and must be approved before registration.
- **Remote work costs:** home internet, electricity, and furniture cannot be claimed as expenses. Staff with an approved hybrid arrangement receive the remote work allowance in VB-POL-013.

## 15. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- personal expenses of any kind, including toiletries and clothing
- travel or meals for spouses, partners, or family members
- traffic and parking fines
- upgrades above entitlement, and first-class travel
- minibar, in-room movies, and spa services
- laundry, except on trips of 5 nights or more
- tips above 10% of the bill
- loss or theft of personal items
- alcohol outside approved client entertainment
- home working costs (see section 14)

## 16. Misuse

False, inflated, or duplicate claims are fraud and gross misconduct under VB-POL-004. Finance refers suspected expense fraud to the Head of Fraud Risk Management, and the bank recovers any amount wrongly paid.
