# Leave and Public Holidays Policy

## 1. Purpose

Rest and time away from work keep staff healthy and effective. For some roles, time away is also a control that helps detect fraud. This policy sets out leave entitlements, how to request leave, the mandatory block leave rules for sensitive roles, and the bank's public holidays.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff members. Fixed-term staff receive entitlements pro rata to the length of their contract. Interns receive 1 day of annual leave for each full month of their internship. Contractors are covered by their own employer's leave terms.

## 3. Leave Year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. All leave is counted in business days, so weekends and public holidays that fall during a period of leave are not deducted.

## 4. Annual Leave

### 4.1 Entitlement

| Band | Annual leave (business days) |
|---|---|
| A | 30 |
| B | 28 |
| C | 25 |
| D | 22 |
| E | 20 |

Staff who have completed 10 years of continuous service receive 3 additional days of annual leave each year.

### 4.2 New joiners and leavers

Annual leave accrues monthly from the start date. Staff who join or leave during the year receive a pro rata entitlement. New staff may take annual leave once they have completed 3 months of service. The line manager may approve earlier leave in an emergency.

### 4.3 Requesting annual leave

- Leave is requested through the HR portal and must be approved by the line manager before it starts.
- Requests for 5 or more business days must be made at least 10 business days in advance. Shorter requests must be made at least 2 business days in advance.
- Branch Managers must keep enough staff to run the branch safely. No more than 25% of a branch's tellers may be on annual leave at the same time.
- Before going on leave, staff hand over their work in writing to the colleague who will cover it.

### 4.4 Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next leave year and must be used by 31 March. Days above that limit are forfeited unless the CHRO approves a larger carry-over because business needs prevented the staff member from taking leave. Unused leave cannot be exchanged for pay, except when a staff member leaves the bank (VB-POL-010).

### 4.5 Recall from annual leave

A line manager may recall a staff member from annual leave only in a genuine emergency and with the approval of the Head of Department. The days not taken are credited back. Reasonable costs of returning, such as a changed travel booking, are reimbursed under the Expense and Business Travel Policy (VB-POL-012).

## 5. Mandatory Block Leave

Staff in sensitive roles, as listed in section 6.5 of the Internal Control Policy (VB-POL-006), must take at least 10 consecutive business days of leave once in each calendar year.

- Mandatory block leave counts toward the annual leave entitlement in section 4.1. It is not additional leave.
- The block must be scheduled in the HR portal by 31 January and taken by 30 November. HR places anyone who has not taken it by 30 November on leave in December.
- The 10 days must all be business days. A block that includes a public holiday must be extended by one day for each holiday.
- The block cannot be split. Staff may take their remaining annual leave at other times.
- A colleague performs the staff member's duties during the block.
- CoreLink and payment system access is suspended for the whole block and restored on the first working day back, after the line manager confirms the staff member's return (VB-POL-002, section 11).
- Staff on mandatory block leave must not be contacted about work or recalled, except with the CRO's approval. Section 4.5 does not apply to mandatory block leave.

## 6. Public Holidays

| New Year's Day | 1 January 2026 | Thursday |
| Heritage Day | 9 March 2026 | Monday |
| Good Friday | 3 April 2026 | Friday |
| Easter Monday | 6 April 2026 | Monday |
| Workers' Day | 1 May 2026 | Friday |
| Independence Day | 15 July 2026 | Wednesday |
| Harvest Day | 28 September 2026 | Monday |
| National Unity Day | 16 October 2026 | Friday |
| Christmas Day | 25 December 2026 | Friday |
| Boxing Day (observed) | 28 December 2026 | Monday |

### 6.2 Rules

- A public holiday that falls on a Saturday or Sunday is observed on the following Monday. In 2026, Boxing Day falls on Saturday 26 December and is observed on Monday 28 December.
- Saturday branches close on any public holiday that falls on a Saturday, including 26 December 2026.
- All branches, head office, and regional offices close on public holidays. Digital channels, digital hubs, the DFS Contact Center, the Fraud Desk, and the SOC continue to operate.
- If the government declares an additional public holiday, HR announces it on the staff intranet and branches close.
- The CHRO publishes the following year's public holiday list by 30 November each year.
- Because business days exclude public holidays, any deadline in a bank policy that is expressed in business days skips public holidays.

### 6.3 Working on public holidays and Saturdays

- Staff rostered to work on a public holiday, for example in the SOC, the DFS Contact Center, the Fraud Desk, or IT on-call, receive a day off in lieu, to be taken within 30 days.
- Branch staff who work on a Saturday at a Saturday branch receive a day off in lieu, to be taken within the following 2 weeks (VB-POL-008).
- A day off in lieu not taken within these periods is forfeited, unless the line manager postponed it for business reasons.

## 7. Sick Leave

- Staff who are unwell must tell their line manager before their normal start time: 7:30 a.m. for branch staff and 8:00 a.m. for head office and regional office staff.
- Staff receive up to 12 business days of paid sick leave each year.
- A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive business days, and for any sick leave taken immediately before or after annual leave or a public holiday.
- Sick leave beyond 12 days is handled as extended sick leave on the recommendation of the bank's medical adviser: up to 60 business days at full pay, followed by up to 60 business days at half pay.
- Medical information is held only by HR and is treated as sensitive personal data under VB-POL-003.

## 8. Family Leave

| Leave type | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Maternity leave | 16 weeks at full pay. It may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected due date. On returning to work, the staff member may take 1 hour a day for nursing for 6 months. |
| Paternity leave | 10 business days at full pay, taken within 3 months of the birth. |
| Adoption leave | 12 weeks at full pay for the primary adopter and 10 business days for the other parent, when adopting a child under 5 years old. |

Family leave is in addition to annual leave. Staff should give HR at least 8 weeks' notice of maternity or adoption leave where possible.

## 9. Other Leave

| Leave type | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Compassionate leave | 5 business days for the death of a spouse, child, parent, or sibling; 2 business days for a grandparent or parent-in-law |
| Marriage leave | 3 business days, once during employment with the bank |
| Examination leave | The day of the exam plus 1 study day before it, for exams in bank-approved professional qualifications |
| Study leave | For bank-sponsored programs, as agreed with the CHRO in the sponsorship agreement |
| Unpaid leave | Up to 30 business days a year, with approval from the Head of Department and the CHRO |

A staff member standing for public office takes unpaid leave for the campaign period (see VB-POL-004, section 14).

## 10. Leave During a Notice Period

Annual leave during a notice period requires the line manager's approval. Unused annual leave is paid when the staff member leaves (VB-POL-010).

## 11. Unauthorized Absence

Absence without approval is unpaid and is handled under the disciplinary procedure in VB-POL-004. Absence for 5 consecutive business days without contacting the bank is treated as job abandonment under VB-POL-010.

## 12. Records

All leave is recorded in the HR portal. Leave records form part of the personnel file, which is kept for 7 years after the staff member leaves (VB-POL-003).
